Care Navigator

Text one number with any medical question. It knows your coverage and location, so it tells you what to do, where to go, and what it will cost.

+1 (646) 620-8124 iMessage demo line. During the hackathon it only answers phones we've added, so ask us for a live demo.

How it works

  1. Set up by text in about two minutes. Send a photo of your insurance card, or say you don't have insurance. Add your household size, an income range, and English or Spanish.
  2. Text any health question. You get first-aid steps and when to go to the ER, then it asks where you are.
  3. Get two or three places open right now. Each one shows distance, hours, what it will cost you, and where the information came from.
  4. It checks in the next day. If you're worse, it points you to a higher level of care.

Same question, three different answers

Kaiser member

The nearest Kaiser urgent care that's open, the Kaiser advice line, and your copay.

Medi-Cal member

An urgent care or community clinic in your plan's network, open now, $0.

No insurance

A sliding-fee clinic at your income, or a cash urgent care with its listed price, and when the ER is worth it.

Safety and privacy

This is not for emergencies. If you think it's an emergency, call 911. For a mental health crisis, call or text 988. Emergency warning signs are caught before anything else and always get that answer.

It never diagnoses. It doesn't keep your member ID or the photo of your card, and it asks for an income range, not an exact number. Text STOP anytime.

Why it's different

Insurer chatbots only help their own members. General chatbots don't know your plan or what's open near you. Care Navigator works with any plan or none, and every option comes from real directory and clinic data with a source link, never made up.

Built at SanD Hacks 2026 for the Bright Data "Data for Good" challenge. Data from Community Health Group's public provider directory, clinic websites (collected with Bright Data), and OpenStreetMap.
